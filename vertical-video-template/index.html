Frequently asked questions

Questions

Why did you make a film of my property?

We select properties that we believe could benefit from a strong cinematic presentation and create the film first, so you can see the actual result before deciding whether you want it.

Where did you get the property photos?

We work from publicly available property marketing materials. Before final delivery, we make sure the property visuals are cleared for use.

Do I have to pay for the film?

No. There is no upfront payment or obligation. You only pay if you decide you want the final film.

What happens if I don’t buy the film?

Nothing. There is no obligation to buy it. If the film isn’t claimed, we automatically delete it after 30 days. You can also ask us to remove it at any time.

Can I request changes to the film?

Yes. If you like the film but want adjustments, you can ask us about changes before final delivery.

What do I receive if I buy it?

You receive the final film without preview restrictions, ready to use for your property marketing.

Can I publish the film on my website and social media?

Yes. Once you claim the film, we deliver a final version ready for commercial use. We use music that is licensed and cleared for commercial publishing, so you can use the finished film on your website, social media, property listings, and marketing channels. Before final delivery, we also make sure the property visuals are cleared for use.

Can I use it in property listings and paid advertising?

Yes, once the final film and its assets are cleared for the intended commercial use.

Was the film made with AI?

We use a combination of AI production tools and professional editing to turn property photography into cinematic motion.

Can you make films for my other properties?

Yes. If you like the result, we can create films for other properties in your portfolio as well.
